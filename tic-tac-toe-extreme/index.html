Privacy Policy — Tic-Tac-Toe-Extreme

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Tic-Tac-Toe-Extreme ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile game.

1. We Do Not Collect or Hold Your Data

All your data stays on your device. Your saved games, the names you enter for players, your theme choice, and your settings are stored locally on your device. We have no servers. We do not collect, hold, transmit, or track any information about you.

2. No Ads, No Analytics, No Accounts

The app shows no advertising and includes no advertising SDK. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. There is no account to create and no sign-in. The app does not request the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it tracks nothing.

3. The App Does Not Use the Internet

Gameplay is entirely offline. The app makes no network requests, so nothing about your use of it leaves your device.

4. How We Use Information

We use no information at all. Local data is used only by the app on your device, to run the game and to let you return to a game you left in progress.

5. Third-Party Services

None. The app integrates no third-party service that receives data about you.

6. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the saved game in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it all. We retain nothing, because we never receive anything.

7. Your Choices

You can delete any saved game from within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all local data it created.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone, as described above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For questions or support, use our Support & Feedback form.
